
# FLUX – Conceptual Layer

FLUX is the conceptual design space of HEX.  
It incubates **principles, conditions, and potential patterns** that later become manifest in LIVE and tested in RUN.

## ❦ Components

✶ **Occurrences**: raw ideas or initial events.  
✶ **Potential-Patterns**: prototypes of flow and transformation.  
✶ **Principles**: construction rules and coherence conditions.

## ❦ Principle

FLUX does not execute or manifest.  
Its role is to **formulate the seeds of transformation**, preparing them to be interpreted and enacted by other layers.

## ❦ Role in the System

✶ **Λ**: axioms  
✶ **FLUX**: principles and patterns  
✶ **LIVE**: perceptive manifestation  
✶ **RUN**: execution and validation  
✶ **Src**: technical implementation  


> _FLUX is where transformations are conceived._  
> _Every principle is a seed._  
> _Every pattern is a possible flow._
